List methods cheatsheet
lst.append(x)Add x to end
lst.insert(i, x)Insert x at index i
lst.remove(x)Remove first occurrence of x
lst.pop(i)Remove & return item at i
lst.extend(iter)Add all items from iterable
lst.sort()Sort list in-place
lst.reverse()Reverse list in-place
lst.index(x)First index of x
lst.count(x)Count occurrences of x
lst.copy()Return a shallow copy
lst.clear()Remove all items
lst[i:j]Slice from i to j
